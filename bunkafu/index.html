What is bunkafu?

Shamisen players share music as bunkafu (文化譜), a tablature written on three lines with numbers marking where to press the string. It's easy to read, but making a clean copy by hand or in a general-purpose music program is slow and awkward. shamiComposer is built for writing it.

Strings and positions

Each line is one of the shamisen's three strings: ichi no ito, the thickest, at the bottom, and san no ito, the thinnest, at the top. A number on a line says which position to press on that string. 0 means the open string. A dot is a rest.

The numbers go up one per half step, with two extra positions marked by a sign instead of a number: # between 3 and 4, and b between 9 and 10. That makes 10 the octave above the open string.

Techniques

Symbols around the numbers mark techniques such as sukui (an upstroke), hajiki (a left-hand pluck), uchi (a hammer-on) and suri (a slide). Shamisen Zentrale's guide is a good introduction to reading it.
